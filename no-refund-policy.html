Our Pass Guarantee & Refund Policy

What happens if you don't pass on your first attempt — and why we don't offer cash refunds.

🛡️
Our Commitment

If You Don't Pass, We Don't Walk Away.

Enrollment in a Delphi program is a commitment from us as much as it is from you. If you complete the coursework and put in the work but don't pass your Comp exam or USMLE Step 1 on your first attempt, we stay with you and continue to guide, teach, and support you until you pass.

  • Continued 1:1 guidance and mentorship from Dr. Roy Lingam and the Delphi team at no additional charge.
  • Ongoing access to our teaching sessions, study plans, and course materials while you prepare to retake the exam.
  • For Delphi AceComp students specifically, your tuition can be credited toward the Delphi Core or Premium program — no questions asked.
💳
Refund Policy

All Tuition Payments Are Final.

Because our programs involve immediate, personalized investment — one-on-one instruction, custom study plans, live teaching sessions, and materials made available to you as soon as you enroll — DelphiUSMLE does not issue refunds for any reason once you have enrolled, including if you do not pass the exam, decide not to complete the program, or change your mind after registering.

Instead of a refund, our Pass Guarantee above is how we stand behind our program: continued support until you pass, not your money back.

✍️
Acknowledgment

What You're Agreeing To When You Register

By submitting the Registration Request form on our Enroll page, you confirm that you have read and understood this policy, and that you agree to it as a condition of enrollment:

✓

I understand that DelphiUSMLE does not offer refunds under any circumstances. If I do not pass my exam, DelphiUSMLE will continue to guide, teach, and support me until I pass, at no additional cost — but no money paid will be refunded.
